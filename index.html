What Books Do Successful People Read to Achieve More?

Explore the books that entrepreneurs, investors and leaders turn to for knowledge, and learn how to build a reading habit of your own.

Success is often associated with discipline, determination, creativity, and the ability to make informed decisions. However, one habit shared by many successful entrepreneurs, business leaders, investors, and influential personalities is a commitment to continuous learning. Reading allows people to explore new ideas, understand different perspectives, and gain knowledge from the experiences of others. This raises an interesting question: what books do successful people read, and how do these books influence their personal and professional lives?

From business strategy and financial management to psychology, leadership, and personal development, successful individuals explore a wide range of subjects. Some prefer biographies that reveal the challenges behind major achievements, while others choose practical books that help them improve productivity, manage businesses, or understand human behavior.

Learning about the reading habits of successful people can help anyone build a more meaningful reading routine. Whether you are starting a business, developing your career, managing money, or simply looking for inspiration, exploring the right books can introduce valuable ideas that support long-term growth.

Why Do Successful People Spend Time Reading?

Many successful individuals recognize that education does not end after school or university. The world continues to change, industries evolve, and new challenges require updated knowledge. Reading provides an affordable and flexible way to keep learning without depending entirely on formal education.

One important benefit of reading is improved decision-making. Books about economics, psychology, leadership, and business explain how different factors influence human behavior and organizational performance. Understanding these subjects can help readers evaluate situations more carefully before making important choices.

Reading also introduces people to experiences beyond their personal environments. A biography may explain how an entrepreneur handled business failures, while a historical book can reveal how major economic and social changes influenced entire societies.

Another reason successful people read is to develop creativity. Exploring unfamiliar subjects can help readers connect different ideas and discover alternative approaches to solving problems.

What Books Do Successful People Read?

Although reading preferences vary, many influential individuals are interested in books that provide practical knowledge, encourage independent thinking, and explain important life lessons. The following titles represent different subjects that are frequently associated with successful people's reading habits.

1. The Intelligent Investor

by Benjamin Graham

Investing requires patience, research, and an understanding of financial risk. The Intelligent Investor is a classic investment book that explains the principles of value investing and long-term financial decision-making.

Warren Buffett has repeatedly praised Benjamin Graham's influence on his investment philosophy. The book introduces important concepts such as market behavior, investment discipline, and the difference between investing and speculation.

It is particularly useful for readers who want to understand how thoughtful financial decisions can support long-term wealth building. Although some examples reflect older market conditions, many of its fundamental investment principles remain relevant.

2. Atomic Habits

by James Clear

Success often develops through small actions repeated consistently over time. Atomic Habits explains how everyday routines influence personal and professional progress.

James Clear discusses how individuals can build positive habits, eliminate unhelpful behaviors, and create environments that support better decisions. The book emphasizes systems and consistency rather than relying entirely on motivation.

Readers who struggle with time management, productivity, or maintaining routines may find its practical suggestions especially helpful.

3. Business Adventures

by John Brooks

Understanding how businesses succeed and fail is essential for entrepreneurs and professionals. Business Adventures explores real corporate stories involving major companies, financial decisions, leadership challenges, and organizational mistakes.

Bill Gates has publicly identified this book as one of his favorite business books. Warren Buffett also recommended it to him, making it a well-known example of a business title appreciated by influential readers.

The book demonstrates that business success involves more than financial resources. Leadership, communication, timing, and organizational decisions can all influence the outcome of a company.

4. Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind

by Yuval Noah Harari

Successful people do not necessarily limit their reading to business and finance. Many also explore history, science, and human behavior to develop a broader understanding of the world.

Sapiens examines the development of human civilization, exploring subjects such as agriculture, culture, religion, technology, and economic systems.

Mark Zuckerberg included this title in his publicly shared reading selections. Its broad historical perspective makes it an interesting choice for readers who enjoy exploring how societies developed and how human cooperation shaped the modern world.

5. Principles: Life and Work

by Ray Dalio

Ray Dalio's Principles: Life and Work explains the personal and professional principles that influenced his approach to decision-making and business management.

The book explores transparency, problem-solving, learning from mistakes, and creating systems that support better organizational decisions.

It is particularly relevant for entrepreneurs, managers, and professionals who want to improve their leadership skills. Readers can also learn how examining past experiences can help them make more informed decisions in the future.

6. Shoe Dog

by Phil Knight

Shoe Dog is the memoir of Nike co-founder Phil Knight. It describes the early challenges involved in building the company and transforming a small business idea into a globally recognized brand.

The book provides insight into entrepreneurship, branding, financial uncertainty, teamwork, and persistence. Rather than presenting business growth as a straightforward process, Knight discusses the risks and difficult experiences behind the company's development.

Aspiring business owners can find valuable lessons in its honest description of the entrepreneurial journey.

Different Types of Books Successful People Prefer

Understanding reading preferences can help readers select books that match their personal goals. Successful people often explore several literary categories rather than focusing on a single subject.

Business and entrepreneurship books

These titles explain how companies operate, how business models develop, and how entrepreneurs manage financial and organizational challenges. They are especially useful for people interested in starting or expanding a business.

Personal development books

Books about habits, productivity, discipline, and emotional intelligence help readers understand their behavior and develop more effective routines.

Financial and investment books

These publications introduce concepts related to saving, investing, budgeting, risk management, and wealth creation. They can help readers become more financially aware.

Biographies and autobiographies

Real-life stories provide insight into the experiences, failures, and achievements of influential individuals. They demonstrate that success often involves overcoming unexpected obstacles.

Psychology and philosophy books

These subjects encourage deeper thinking about human behavior, relationships, decision-making, and personal values.

History and science books

Understanding historical events and scientific discoveries can improve general knowledge and encourage readers to consider different perspectives.

How Reading Can Improve Your Personal and Professional Life

Reading can contribute to personal development in several ways. One of its most valuable benefits is the ability to improve knowledge gradually. Spending a small amount of time reading every day can introduce useful information that becomes increasingly valuable over time.

Books about leadership can improve communication and help readers understand how to work with different personalities. Business publications can explain market behavior, customer expectations, and organizational challenges.

Financial literature can also encourage more responsible money management. Readers can learn about budgeting, investment principles, financial risks, and the importance of long-term planning.

Another advantage is improved concentration. Reading requires sustained attention, which can help people become more comfortable with activities that demand patience and thoughtful analysis.

However, reading alone does not guarantee success. The most meaningful results come when readers evaluate the information, apply relevant lessons, and adjust their actions based on experience.

How to Build a Reading Habit Like Successful People

Developing a reading routine does not require expensive books or several free hours every day. A simple and consistent approach can make reading easier to maintain.

Start by choosing books that match your current interests. If you are interested in business, begin with an accessible entrepreneurship title. If you want to improve your habits, choose a personal development book.

Set aside 15 to 30 minutes each day for reading. Morning hours or the time before sleeping can be suitable because they provide an opportunity to focus without unnecessary distractions.

Keep a notebook to record important ideas, useful examples, and questions. After finishing each book, identify a few lessons that you can realistically apply to your daily routine.

You can also explore audiobooks and digital reading platforms if physical books are not convenient. The important thing is to develop a consistent learning habit rather than focusing only on the number of books completed.

Conclusion

Understanding what books successful people read provides an excellent opportunity to discover valuable knowledge and explore the ideas that influence influential individuals. From the investment principles in The Intelligent Investor to the practical habits discussed in Atomic Habits, different books offer lessons that can support personal and professional development.

Reading preferences vary among entrepreneurs, investors, innovators, and business leaders. Some focus on financial knowledge, while others explore history, psychology, leadership, and creative thinking. This variety demonstrates that continuous learning can come from many different subjects.

However, simply reading popular books does not guarantee achievement. Real progress comes from understanding useful ideas, applying them consistently, and learning from personal experiences. Choose books that match your goals, develop a regular reading schedule, and make learning a lifelong habit.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What books do successful people read most often?

Successful people explore various genres, including business, finance, psychology, biographies, history, and personal development. Popular examples include Atomic Habits, The Intelligent Investor, Business Adventures, and Principles: Life and Work.

2. Which books does Warren Buffett recommend?

Warren Buffett has frequently praised The Intelligent Investor by Benjamin Graham. He also recommended Business Adventures by John Brooks to Bill Gates, who later described it as one of his favorite business books.

3. What books are best for becoming a successful entrepreneur?

Entrepreneurs can benefit from reading Shoe Dog by Phil Knight, The Lean Startup by Eric Ries, and Business Adventures by John Brooks. These books explore business development, innovation, leadership, and real-world corporate experiences.

4. Do successful people read fiction books?

Yes. Many successful individuals enjoy fiction alongside business and educational literature. Fiction can encourage creativity, improve imagination, and introduce readers to different human experiences.

5. How many books should I read to become successful?

There is no specific number of books required for success. Reading one or two meaningful books each month can be a good starting point. Understanding and applying useful information matters more than reading speed.

6. Which books help improve financial knowledge?

The Intelligent Investor by Benjamin Graham and The Psychology of Money by Morgan Housel are popular choices. They introduce investment principles, financial behavior, risk, and long-term money management.

7. Why do entrepreneurs read books regularly?

Entrepreneurs read to improve their knowledge, understand business challenges, explore new ideas, and develop better decision-making skills. Reading also allows them to learn from other people's experiences.

8. Can beginners follow the reading habits of successful people?

Absolutely. Beginners can start with accessible books such as Atomic Habits and The Psychology of Money. Gradually exploring more advanced subjects can help develop confidence and a stronger understanding of different topics.
